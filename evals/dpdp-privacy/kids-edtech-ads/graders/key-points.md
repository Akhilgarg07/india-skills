PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. Says users under 18 are children under the DPDP Act, so verifiable consent of a parent/lawful guardian is required before processing their data (e.g. via reliable identity/age details or a virtual token such as via DigiLocker).
2. Says tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children are prohibited, so the personalised-ads plan cannot go ahead for these users.
3. Mentions that exemptions are limited and specified (e.g. for certain educational or safety purposes) and do not cover advertising, and/or the penalty of up to ₹200 crore.
4. Includes a not-legal-advice caveat.

FAIL if the answer permits targeted ads to under-18 users with parental consent.
