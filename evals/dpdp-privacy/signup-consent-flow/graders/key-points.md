PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. Replaces the pre-ticked bundled checkbox with separate, unticked, per-purpose consents (e.g. service vs marketing vs partner sharing), because consent must be free, specific, informed, unambiguous and given by a clear affirmative action.
2. Includes a standalone, plain-language notice with an itemised list of the personal data and the specific purposes, plus how to withdraw consent, exercise rights and complain to the Data Protection Board.
3. Makes withdrawal as easy as giving consent, and propagates withdrawal to processors.
4. Provides a consent record/log design (notice version, purpose, timestamp, channel, withdrawals) so the company can prove notice and consent.
5. States it is not legal advice and/or notes the phased commencement (most Rules obligations apply 18 months after the November 2025 notification).

FAIL if the design keeps a single bundled or pre-ticked consent.
