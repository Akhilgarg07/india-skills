PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. Says each affected Data Principal must be informed without delay, in plain language, via their account or registered contact, including what happened, likely consequences, mitigation, steps they can take, and a contact person.
2. Says the Data Protection Board must get an initial intimation without delay and a detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware (unless the Board allows longer on written request).
3. Notes that the DPDP framework has no materiality threshold (any personal data breach must be reported), and mentions penalties up to ₹200 crore for failure to notify.
4. Mentions other reporting regimes (e.g. CERT-In or sector regulators) may also apply, or says it is not legal advice.

FAIL if the answer says notification is optional or gives only a 72-hour deadline to users.
