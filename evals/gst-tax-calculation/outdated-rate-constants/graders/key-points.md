PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. States that since 22 September 2025 the main GST rates are 5% and 18% with a special 40% rate, and that 12% and 28% no longer apply to almost all supplies.
2. Recommends storing rates as data keyed by HSN/SAC code and effective date (not hard-coded slabs by category).
3. Notes that older invoices must keep the rate that applied at their time of supply.
4. Includes or recommends confirming against the official rate notification, or says it is not tax advice.

FAIL if the answer accepts 12% or 28% as current general slabs or does not mention the 40% rate.
