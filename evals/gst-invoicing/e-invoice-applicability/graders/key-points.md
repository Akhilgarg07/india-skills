1. Says e-invoicing applies because aggregate turnover exceeded ₹5 crore in at least one financial year from 2017-18 onwards (it does not switch off because last year was lower).

4. Mentions at least one operational rule correctly: IRN cancellation only within 24 hours, or the 30-day reporting limit applying to AATO of ₹10 crore or more.
