PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. Says the ₹30,000 single-payment limit is not the only test: TDS applies once the aggregate for the financial year exceeds ₹1,00,000.
2. Uses the 2% rate because the payee is a firm (1% applies only to individuals/HUFs).
3. Says that when the aggregate is crossed (the 5th payment, ₹1,25,000 total), TDS is due on the whole aggregate including the earlier untaxed payments (2% of ₹1,25,000 = ₹2,500).
4. Recommends tracking cumulative payments per payee per financial year in code, and mentions consequences of non-deduction (interest and/or 30% disallowance).

FAIL if the answer agrees that no TDS is needed.
