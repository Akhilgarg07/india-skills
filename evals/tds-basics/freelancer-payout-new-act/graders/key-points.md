PASS only if the response does ALL of the following:
1. Says the Income-tax Act 2025 applies because the payment/credit is after 1 April 2026, and the section to quote is section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(iii)] (the old section 194J must not be quoted).
2. Excludes the separately shown GST from the TDS base, so TDS is on ₹80,000, not ₹94,400.
3. Applies the right rate: 10% (₹8,000) if it is professional services, or 2% (₹1,600) if it is fees for technical services, and explains the distinction or the need to classify.
4. Notes that the ₹50,000 annual threshold is exceeded, and includes a not-tax-advice caveat.

FAIL if the answer only cites section 194J for a June 2026 payment, or computes TDS on the GST-inclusive amount.
