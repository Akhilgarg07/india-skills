# Sources: indian-addresses

**Last verified: 2026-10-18**

Method key: **[page]** = official page/PDF downloaded and read; **[extract]** = official URL not
downloadable from our environment, fact taken from a search-engine extract of it.

## States and UTs
- [extract] Ministry of Home Affairs, State and UT police list (28 States) and AGMUT cadre page
  (8 UTs; legislatures in Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu & Kashmir).
  https://www.mha.gov.in/en/commoncontent/state-and-uts-police ,
  https://mha.gov.in/index.php/en/division_of_mha/AGMUT_Cadre_Management
- [extract] PIB, maps of the new UTs of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh (reorganised 31 Oct 2019; Ladakh =
  Kargil and Leh districts). https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1590112
- [page] GST state code list (incl. "Daman and Diu from July 2017 to 26th Jan 2020", merged UT code 26,
  Telangana 36, AP 37, Ladakh 38). https://nagalandtax.nic.in/docs/GST/Statistics/GST_State_Code_List.pdf

## PIN codes
- [page] Department of Posts, *Golden Jubilee of PINCODE* brochure (introduced 15 Aug 1972; digit
  meanings; 9 PIN regions; one delivery post office per PIN).
  https://postagestamps.gov.in/Pdf/BROCHURE_GOLDEN_JUB_PINCODE.pdf
- [page] PIB release on the Supreme Court PIN code ("first 8 are geographical regions and the digit 9
  is reserved for the Army Postal Service"). https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=99700
- [extract] India Post (Uttarakhand circle) PIN page: first-digit regions and first-two-digit circle
  table (dated; old state names). http://www.upost.gov.in/pincode.htm
- [page] IRIS IRP (GSTN e-invoice IRP) release note, 28 Jan 2025: Chandigarh mapped to PINs starting
  140 and 160 in the PIN-state mapping.
  https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/e-invoice-mandate-e-invoicing-changes-exemptions-documents-covered-transactions-and-more/
- [extract] All India Pincode Directory on data.gov.in (official dataset; download blocked from our
  environment). https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/all-india-pincode-directory-through-webservice
- [extract] India Post DIGIPIN technical document and FAQ (10-character code, about 4 m × 4 m grid,
  complements the PIN). https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/intiatives/DIGIPIN_Technical_document.pdf
