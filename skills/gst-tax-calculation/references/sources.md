## Rate structure (22 Sep 2025 onwards)
- [page] GST Council / PIB, Recommendations of the 56th GST Council meeting (3 Sep 2025):
  "a 2 rate structure with a Standard Rate of 18% and a Merit Rate of 5%; a special de-merit
  rate of 40% for a select few goods and services", plus item-level changes.
  https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/press_release_press_information_bureau_0.pdf
- [page] GST Council FAQs on the 56th meeting (effective 22 Sep 2025; tobacco exceptions;
  cars, motorcycles, coal, passenger transport 5% without ITC).
  https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/faq_0.pdf
- [page] PIB FAQs on the 56th meeting. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163560
- [extract] Notification 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17 Sep 2025, Schedules I-VII at
  CGST 2.5%, 9%, 20%, 1.5%, 0.125%, 0.75%, 14% (confirmed by a GST Council press release
  citing "Schedule I ... Schedule II of notification No. 09/2025-CTR dated 17.09.2025").
  IGST counterpart 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate): 5%, 18%, 40%, 3%, 0.25%, 1.5%, 28%.
  https://courier.cbic.gov.in/ECCS/advisory/2025/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%209_2025-INTEGRATED%20TAX%20(RATE)%20-1759486719.pdf
- [page] GST Council Newsletter, December 2025: Notification 19/2025-CT(Rate) dated 31 Dec 2025
  (tobacco and pan masala to Schedules II/III, Schedule VII omitted, from 1 Feb 2026) and
  03/2025-Compensation Cess (Rate) (cess Nil from 1 Feb 2026); RSP-based valuation.
  https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/monthly_newsletter_december_2025_1_0.pdf
- [page] Recommendations of the 57th GST Council meeting (8 Oct 2026): mainly process reforms;
  no change to the slab structure. https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/press_release.pdf
