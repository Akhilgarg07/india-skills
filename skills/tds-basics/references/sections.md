# TDS sections: old Act vs new Act, and details

> Not tax advice. Rates/thresholds as retained in the Income-tax Act 2025 per the CBDT /
> e-filing portal TDS transition FAQ. Verify against the current Act and Rules.

## Transition rules (e-filing portal "TDS Compliance" FAQ)

1. **Governing Act** = Act in force on the **earlier of credit or payment**.
   - Fees credited in March 2026, paid in April 2026 → old Act, deduct in March 2026.
   - Advance paid in March 2026, credited in April 2026 → old Act.
   - Monthly contract: March 2026 credit → s.194C; April 2026 credit → s.393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(i)].
     No need to amend the contract.
2. **Salary**: tax is deducted at payment, so March 2026 salary paid on 31 Mar 2026 → old
   Act; April 2026 salary paid 30 Apr 2026 → new Act. The employer resets the computation
   from 1 Apr 2026 for Tax Year 2026-27.
3. **No rate or threshold changes**: "The TDS rates and monetary thresholds for all
   categories of payments have been retained as they are under the Income Tax Act, 1961."
4. **Section codes**: quoting the old section for a new-Act payment "may lead to processing
   errors"; a correction statement may be needed.
5. **Corrections** for old-Act periods use old-Act forms, filed within two years from the
   end of the tax year in which the original statement was due.
6. **Challan-cum-statements**: Form 26QB (property, 194-IA), 26QC (rent by individuals, 194-IB),
   26QD (194M) and 26QE (VDA, 194S) continue for old-Act events. From 1 Apr 2026 one common
   **Form 141** (Income-tax Rules 2026) covers all four.
7. **TCS** consolidated into **section 394**, with the same transition principle.
8. **Credit for the deductee**: March 2026 deductions → AY 2026-27 (AIS); April 2026 onwards
   → Tax Year 2026-27 (Form 168 annual information statement).
9. **Old circulars** remain valid where not inconsistent with the new Act (s.536(2)(j)). For
   example, the circular on the meaning of "work" under s.194C applies to s.393.

## Contractor (194C / 393 Sl. No. 6(i))
- Payer: specified persons (companies, firms, government, etc., and individuals/HUFs whose
  previous-year turnover exceeds ₹1 crore for business or ₹50 lakh for profession).
- Rate: 1% (individual/HUF payee), 2% (others).
- Thresholds: single sum ≤ ₹30,000 and aggregate for the FY ≤ ₹1,00,000 → no deduction
  (₹1,00,000 aggregate since 1 Jun 2016).

## Professional / technical fees (194J / 393 Sl. No. 6(iii))
- 2%: fees for technical services (not professional services); royalty for sale, distribution or
  exhibition of cinematographic films; payee engaged only in operating a call centre.
- 10%: other cases (professional fees, other royalty, etc.).
- Threshold raised from ₹30,000 to **₹50,000** from 1 Apr 2025 (Finance Act 2025). Once
  exceeded, TDS applies on the entire amount.
- Deduct on the gross bill including reimbursements (CBDT Circular 715/1995). GST shown
  separately is excluded (Circular 23/2017).

## No PAN (206AA / 397(2))
Rate = highest of: the rate in the provision; the rates in force; **20%** (but **5%** for the
buyer under Sl. No. 8(ii) and the e-commerce operator under Sl. No. 8(v)).
Section 206AB (higher rate for non-filers) was omitted with effect from 1 Apr 2025.

## Deposit due dates
- Non-government deductors: by the 7th of the following month; deductions in **March: by 30 April**.
- Government offices: same day if paid without challan; otherwise within 7 days of month-end.
- Interest: 1% per month for failure to deduct; 1.5% per month for failure to deposit after deduction.
